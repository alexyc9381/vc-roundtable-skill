# Demo run

`/roundtable --demo --seed 7`, run headless in Claude Code on Oct 24, 2026, on a made-up meal-planning startup (`input.txt`, `about.txt`). A headless run has no one to ask, so the answers to step 1 were given in the prompt, and the grilling was answered from the pitch and facts only. Nothing was said about what the result should be.

What happened:
- At the table: Y Combinator, Sequoia, a16z, General Catalyst, Index Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Benchmark.
- 14 grilling questions, then the vote: **0 fund, 1 fund if (Y Combinator), 6 pass**. No vote was thrown out.
- 5 of 7 firms quoted the same slide: "Traction. 400 families on the waitlist. 60 families paying $8 a month since August. 41 of the 60 are still paying."
- Y Combinator's fix: call all 19 families who quit, ship one fix within a week, and show what happened to the rest.
- The coach's new first line: "Since August, 60 families have paid us $8 a month to plan their dinners and fill their grocery cart every Sunday."

Everything the table wrote is in `roundtable-output/`: the case, the questions, the answers, every vote, the checked votes, the tally, the coach's notes and `REPORT.md`.

The firms are Claudes playing each firm's public investing advice. They are not the real firms, and no firm made or endorses this.
